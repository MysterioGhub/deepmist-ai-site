Ramadan & Eid

Free Ramadan hooks for vertical video ads

These are opening lines for short vertical ads that run during Ramadan and Eid: iftar, gifting, Eid preparation and late evenings. They are free to use in your own ads, with no sign-up. If you would rather we make the ad, every frame we deliver is AI-generated, and we say so wherever it runs.

Planning note Expected to begin on or around 8 February 2027 (Umm al-Qura calendar). The actual first day is confirmed by moon sighting and can differ by a day between countries. Eid al-Fitr is expected on or around 9 March 2027, also subject to moon sighting.

Ramadan starts with the sighting of the new moon, so the exact start is only confirmed shortly before. The month is also busy for your team and for everyone you depend on. Creative made in a rush tends to look like every other brand's. Brief it early, and leave room to adjust.

The hooks

Iftar and hosting

For food, dates, coffee and anything that helps a family host well at sunset.

  • This is what's on our table when the whole family comes for iftar.
  • I used to stress about hosting. Now I prepare this before sunset.
  • The dates my grandmother would actually approve of.
  • Everyone asks where I got this when they come over after maghrib.

Gifting

For oud, perfume, sweets and hampers people give to family, friends and colleagues.

  • If you still don't know what to bring when you visit, watch this.
  • The gift that gets opened at the gathering, not saved for later.
  • I wrapped this for my mother and she asked where I found it.
  • A Ramadan gift that feels thoughtful without trying too hard.

Eid preparation

For outfits, beauty, fragrance and getting the home ready for guests.

  • My Eid outfit is sorted, and I never set foot in a mall.
  • Getting the house guest-ready for Eid, the easy way.
  • What I always buy before Eid morning.
  • Eid photos with the whole family. Here's how I get ready.

Evenings and suhoor

For late-night shopping, comfort, family time and the quiet hours before dawn.

  • What I order after taraweeh when the house finally goes quiet.
  • Late nights in Ramadan feel different. This makes them easier.
  • Suhoor at our house never looks like this. Until now.
  • Shopping for Eid at midnight, from the sofa, with the family.

Writing for the month

Respect the month

Lead with family, generosity and hosting. Keep the product in service of the moment, never above it, and leave religious phrases out of the sales line.

Mind the daylight

Avoid showing people eating or drinking during the day. Food shots belong at iftar or suhoor, when a fasting viewer will welcome them.

Write Arabic natively

A Ramadan ad translated from English reads as foreign to a Gulf viewer. Write the Arabic script on its own terms, with its own warmth and idiom.

Give, not just discount

Price offers are everywhere this month. An ad about the gift, the table or the guest tends to stand apart from a wall of percentages.
