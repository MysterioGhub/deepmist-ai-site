Gulf sale season

Free White Friday hooks for sale-season video ads.

Opening lines for the Gulf's big sale season, grouped by the kind of ad you are making: the honest deal, the gift run, the haul and the upgrade. They are free to use in your own ads, with no sign-up. If you want us to make the ad, every frame is AI-generated and disclosed.

Planning note In 2025 the big Gulf marketplace sales ran roughly 20 to 30 November. Each retailer announces its own 2026 dates, often only shortly before, so confirm them on the retailer's own site before you book media.

Sale-season feeds fill with the same shouted discount at the same moment. An ad that sounds like a person explaining why a deal is worth it stands apart from that noise. Brief it before the retailers announce their dates, so the creative is ready and checked instead of rushed out the night before.

The hooks

The honest deal

For brands that want the sale to sound like advice, not a siren.

  • I waited all year to buy this at a fair price.
  • Here is what I would actually buy in this sale, and why.
  • Before you fill your cart, ask if you will really use it.
  • This was on my list long before it went on sale.

The gift run

Year-end gifting starts in the sale season. These suit perfume, sweets, gadgets and gift sets.

  • Sorting my gift list while the prices are kind.
  • The gift everyone in my family keeps asking about.
  • Buying for someone who has everything? Start here.
  • I bought this as a gift and kept it for myself.

The haul

For fashion, beauty and home brands showing what arrived and how it looks out of the box.

  • Here is what came in the sale box.
  • Everything in this sale order, unboxed piece by piece.
  • Unboxing the pieces I had been saving for all season.
  • Let me show you how this pairs with what you already own.

The upgrade

For tech, appliances and furniture, where buyers wait for the sale to replace something old.

  • Mine finally gave up, so the sale timing works.
  • Replacing something old? Here is what to look for.
  • What to check before you upgrade during the sale.
  • The upgrade I kept putting off, done properly this time.

Writing for the sale season

Keep prices factual

Say a price is reduced only if the retailer's listing shows it. Never invent a discount, a former price or a deadline in the ad.

A reason, not a siren

Viewers scroll past shouting. A calm reason to buy, from someone who seems to use the product, holds attention where another flashing banner does not.

Arabic written natively

Gulf viewers hear a translated script straight away. Write the Arabic version on its own terms instead of converting the English line.

Ready before the dates

Retailers announce their sale dates late. Have the creative made and checked early, then drop in the confirmed price and dates when they land.
