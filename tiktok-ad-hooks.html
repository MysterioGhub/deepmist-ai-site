Shooting a hook for TikTok

Start mid-moment

Open with the product already in hand and the line already being said. A slow fade-in or a logo sting gives the thumb time to move on.

Sound like a post

Write the hook the way a person captions their own video, not the way a brand writes a slogan. If it would look odd in a friend's feed, rewrite it.

Show it working

The lines here promise a look at something. Keep that promise on screen straight away: the product doing its job, filmed close and upright.

Label it as AI

An ad with an AI presenter should say so, in the ad tools and in the caption. The policy notes below cover the disclaimer TikTok's ad tools ask for.
