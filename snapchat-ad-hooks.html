Shooting a hook for Snapchat

Talk to the lens

Film it the way people film each other: phone held upright, face close, speaking straight into the camera. A studio set reads as an ad before a word lands.

Line before logo

Open on the person and the line. Save the brand card for the end, where the viewers who stayed are ready to see it.

Write it to be read

Some people watch with the sound down. Put the hook on screen as a caption too, word for word, so it lands either way.

Keep it plain

A hook that over-promises is the part of an ad most likely to mislead. Say what the product does, and let the rest of the ad show it.
