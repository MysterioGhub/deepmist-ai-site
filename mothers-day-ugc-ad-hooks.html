Mother’s Day

Free Mother's Day hooks for gift video ads.

Opening lines for brands selling gifts around Mother's Day in the UAE and the Gulf, grouped by the kind of ad you are making: perfume and oud, sweets and flowers, whoever raised you, and the gift sent home. They are free to use in your own ads, with no sign-up. If you want us to make the ad, every frame is AI-generated and disclosed.

Planning note The UAE marks Mother's Day on 21 March every year, as do many countries across the Arab world. It is not the same day everywhere, and the UK and the US keep other dates, so check which day your audience keeps before you schedule a gift ad.

Around the day, feeds fill with the same bouquet and the same greeting. An ad that shows a real gift moment, like a visit, a parcel being opened or a table set for the family, reads as warmer than another card. Brief it early, so the creative is made and checked well before the day instead of rushed.

The hooks

Perfume and oud

For perfume, oud and bakhoor brands whose bottle becomes the gift people remember.

  • She still wears the scent she wore when I was small.
  • The bottle I chose because it smells like her house.
  • My mother has opinions about oud, and this bottle passed.
  • How I picked a perfume for someone who hates surprises.

Sweets and flowers

For chocolate, dates, cake and flower brands that sit on the table when the family visits.

  • I asked my grandmother what she wanted. She said nothing. So, this.
  • The box of dates that is gone before the coffee is poured.
  • Flowers fade fast. This tray lasts the whole visit.
  • The cake my aunt pretends she did not want.

Whoever raised you

Not everyone is buying for a mother. These lines fit grandmothers, aunts, sisters and the people who stood in.

  • This is for my grandmother, who raised all of us.
  • My aunt never missed a school play. This is my thank you.
  • For my older sister, who looked after all of us.
  • A thank you for the person who always picked up the phone.

Sent home

For gift, beauty and delivery brands serving people in the Gulf whose family lives somewhere else.

  • My mother lives far away, so the gift has to do the talking.
  • I cannot be there in person, so this is going instead.
  • What I send home when a video call is not enough.
  • The parcel my mother opens on camera for the whole family.

Writing for Mother's Day

Write for every viewer

For some people this day is hard. Write lines that also fit grandmothers, aunts and the people who stood in, and never push a viewer to buy out of pressure.

Show the gift, not a greeting

A card-style greeting on screen looks like the rest of the feed. Show the product in a real moment, like a visit, a parcel being opened or a table set for the family.

Arabic written natively

Gulf viewers hear a translated script straight away. Write the Arabic version on its own terms instead of converting the English line.

Ready before the day

Gift ads take time to make and check. Have the creative ready well before the day, and confirm which date your audience keeps, since it differs by country.
