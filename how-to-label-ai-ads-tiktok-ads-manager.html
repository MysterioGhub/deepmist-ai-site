How to label an AI-generated ad in TikTok Ads Manager

Our TikTok hook page tells you to switch on the AI-generated content label. This page shows where it lives, using TikTok's own words: each step below is quoted from TikTok's help page, linked and dated, in the order TikTok gives it. We do not run your ad account. This is a plain reading, not legal advice.

Does it apply to your ad?

TikTok's page starts by asking whether the toggle applies to your ad at all. Its conditions, quoted:

“The below conditions must all be met for the ad:”

  • “Is a TikTok ad”
  • “Is not a Spark ad”
  • “Content includes image, video, or audio that is either completely AI-generated or significantly modified by AI.”

Where the toggle is

Then, in TikTok's words, where the toggle lives and how to switch it on:

TikTok: advertisers should “use the AI-generated content disclaimer feature in TikTok Ads Manager under the Ad creation part of the campaign creation process”

  1. “Create a campaign or go to an existing campaign.”
  2. “Under the ad creation process, check the toggle This ad contains AI-generated content”
  3. “Click Submit after you are done with the ad creation process.”

The notes TikTok adds under the steps, quoted. These are the ones that catch people out:

“After you submit, you cannot change the toggle or unselect it unless you change the video or image.”
“Any new campaign creation, including campaign duplication, will reset the AI-generated content disclaimer toggle, even if you reuse an ad that you previously used this toggle for.”

From the top of the same page:

“You might not see the feature for adding disclaimers if the feature is not available to you.”

And on Spark Ads:

“As Spark ads live organically on TikTok, they must follow the TikTok Community Guidelines policy for AI-generated content disclosure.”

Our readingTikTok's help page describes a toggle in Ads Manager for ads that contain AI-generated content, set while the ad is being created. Spark Ads follow TikTok's creator rules for AI content instead.

Worth knowing

Duplicated campaigns

A copied campaign does not keep the toggle, per the note above. Switch it on again in every new or duplicated campaign before you submit.

Toggle missing?

TikTok's page says the disclaimer feature might not be available to every account and points you to your TikTok sales representative. We cannot see or change what your account offers.

Say it in the caption too

The toggle adds TikTok's label. Our own house view: an ad with an AI presenter should also say so in the caption, in plain words, so nobody mistakes the presenter for a customer.

Questions we get asked

Do I need the AI label on an ad you make?

Our ads are fully AI-generated: the presenter is synthetic. TikTok's conditions above cover content that is completely AI-generated, so plan to switch the toggle on. Whether it applies to your campaign is your call, and this is not legal advice.

Can I add the label after the ad is live?

TikTok's note above says that after you submit, the toggle cannot be changed unless you change the video or image. Set it while you create the ad.

What about Spark Ads?

TikTok's page sends Spark Ads to its Community Guidelines on AI-generated content disclosure instead of this toggle. Our platform guide quotes the creator labelling rule.
