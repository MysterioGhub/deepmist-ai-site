AI Video Ads vs an agency on cost

This page compares the cost of hiring a creative or video agency for a short-form ad against commissioning AI video ads from us. We sell the AI side, and we are saying that up front. What follows is the honest version, including where an agency is the better buy and we lose.

We sell one of these two things. So we have written down exactly where the other one wins.

The short answer

If you need an original creative idea built by people who study your market, real filmed footage of your product, and a named team accountable across a whole campaign, hire the agency. If you need several hooks and variations to test cheaply on paid social, and your product can carry a synthetic presenter, AI production is the better buy. Most brands need each route at different times.

Where this comparison stops flattering us

Not every agency is a full-service one. A performance marketing agency — template-based editing, modular assembly, no custom shoot — is quoted at $100–$500 per video ad in the same public benchmark, turning work around in 1–5 days. That overlaps our own prices. So “AI is cheaper than an agency” is not true across the board: it is true against shoot-based production, and roughly a wash against a performance shop that is already working from your existing assets. If that is the lane you are in, price both and pick on the work, not the invoice.

When to hire an agency

Original brand strategy

When the problem is positioning, not production, an agency builds an original idea and a point of view on your category. We render your idea, not invent your brand.

The product is proof

Your claim may need a real pour, a real fit, or real water resistance. Filmed footage carries that proof; a synthetic presenter only asks the viewer to trust it.

Cross-channel campaigns

When a launch runs across feed, retail, and broadcast, you want a named partner owning it. Coordinating that many surfaces is real work, and not work we take on.

High-stakes launches

When a misfire can damage the brand, you want a named team accountable and work built to outlast a test cycle. That accountability costs, and it is worth paying for.

When AI production is the better buy

Testing many hooks

When you need to try several hooks before committing spend, AI renders variations off the same pack. A shoot means a fresh setup for every idea you want to test.

Cost per variation

When the math is cost per variation, AI wins outright. Each extra hook is another render, not another shoot day with crew and kit to pay for.

No shoot logistics

When there is no appetite to coordinate crew, locations, and talent, AI removes it entirely. We work from your product photos, so a shoot never sits on the critical path.

Reaching new markets

When you want to run in a language or market where a shoot is impractical, AI reaches it from the same pack, without rebuilding the production per locale.
