What happens inside the delivery time

What happens between your order and the delivery, and which parts depend on you.

  1. Your brief is read

    We read your package, your answers and your photos. If the order does not say who your ideal customer is, we ask in the order chat, and that wait comes out of the delivery window.

  2. The script is written and sent to you

    The hook, the shots and the spoken lines are written from your brief and sent to you on the order page before any video is made.

  3. The video is made

    The presenter, the product shots and the voice are generated from the approved script, then checked before anything is sent.

  4. Your label is shown to you

    If the label text on your product had to be recreated, we send you a still of how it reads before delivering the video.

  5. Revisions after delivery

    Revisions come after the delivery. You ask through the revision button on your order, we redo what you name and deliver it again.

  6. Whether to pay for the extra-fast add-on

    Where a package offers it, the extra-fast add-on is chosen at checkout for an extra fee. Whether the earlier date is worth that fee is your call.

Questions we get asked

Which delivery time applies to my order?

The time Fiverr shows on the package at checkout, with the extra-fast add-on if you chose it. Fiverr shows the due date on your order page. The times on this page are read from the same packages.

Can I get my video sooner?

If a package offers the extra-fast add-on, the table above shows its delivery time and its fee. If the table shows none for a package, that package has no earlier option.

Does waiting for my script approval count?

On the services that wait for your approval, yes: production starts only when you approve, so a prompt reply keeps your date. On the others, production goes ahead with the script you were sent if you do not reply.

What if you need more information from me?

We ask in the order chat on Fiverr. The time it takes to get an answer comes out of the delivery window, so the brief checklist below lists what to send with your order.

How do revisions work?

After delivery, you ask for changes through the revision button on your order. We redo what you name and deliver again. Each package lists how many revisions it includes.
