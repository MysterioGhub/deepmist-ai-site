What changes the price

How many videos

The packs cost more in total and less per video. If you plan to test several ads, the price per video column is the figure to compare.

Hook variants

A pack can keep the same concept and change only the opening line, which is how you learn which hook stops the scroll before you spend on media.

Language

Arabic work is priced as its own service because it carries an Arabic voice and Arabic captions, not an English ad with subtitles added.

Length and format

Spokesperson videos run longer than a short UGC ad and include brand elements such as your logo, so they are priced separately.

What the price does not cover

Ad spend is separate

These prices pay for making the video. The budget you spend showing it on TikTok, Meta or Snapchat is paid to that platform, not to us.

Your total is at checkout

Fiverr shows the final amount, including anything Fiverr itself adds, at checkout before you pay. That figure is what counts.

Prices can change

We rebuild this page from our live gigs, but the gig page on Fiverr is always the price you actually pay. If they ever disagree, the gig page is right.

Our packages are priced on Fiverr in US dollars. Fiverr can show them in dirhams and other currencies; the dirham figures here come from Fiverr's own display on the day we read it, rounded.
