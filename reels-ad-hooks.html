Shooting a hook for Reels

Show it in the frame

Every line here points at something visible. Put that thing on screen while the line is said, filmed close and upright, so the picture answers the hook.

Assume the sound is off

Put the hook on screen as text as well as in the voice, placed clear of the caption and the buttons along the edge of the frame.

Pay it off in the clip

The hook promises a reveal. Deliver it inside the video, not in the caption or behind a link, so nobody has to go looking for the answer.

Label it as AI

An ad with an AI presenter should say so, in the caption and wherever the ad tools offer a label. The policy notes below cover what Meta's help pages say about AI labels.

What Meta says about AI in ads

Our platform guide quotes Meta's help pages word for word, with links and the dates we read them. Our reading, in brief:

Meta's help centre serves this article in more than one version, and they disagree about outside AI tools. The en-gb version labels ad images made with Meta's own AI tools and gives ads made with outside AI tools as an example that won't get AI info. The version in Meta's Arabic help centre, also in English, says ad images made with third-party AI tools get AI info too. We can't tell which applies to your account, so plan for the label either way. The advertiser disclosure both describe is for ads about social issues, elections or politics.
