Before you order

What to send before you order an AI UGC video ad

Before you order an AI-generated video ad from us, this is the brief that lets the script be written straight away: what to attach, what to tell us about your customer, and the decisions only you can make. Nothing here needs a designer or a copywriter. Plain answers are enough, and you can paste the checklist straight into your order.

The checklist

  1. Product photos

    Clear photos of the product itself, attached to the order on Fiverr.

    The product in the video is built from your photos, so the item on screen is yours. Sharp, well-lit shots of the label and the shape help most.

  2. What it is and what it does

    A plain line naming the product and the problem it solves.

    The hook and the script are written from this line. Say it the way you would say it to a customer.

  3. Who it is for

    Your ideal customer: their age range, where they live and what they care about.

    The presenter and the voice are matched to this customer. If the order does not say, we ask in the order chat, and that wait comes out of the delivery window.

  4. Where the ad will run

    TikTok, Instagram Reels, Snapchat, YouTube Shorts or a product page.

    The opening seconds and the pacing are shaped for the place it runs.

  5. Hooks you like (optional)

    Any opening lines you want tried, or picks from our free hook library.

    Your picks are the starting point for the hook. Leave this blank and the hook is written from your brief.

  6. Lines to say, and words to avoid

    Anything the ad must say, such as your brand name or your offer, and anything it must not.

    These are read from your order requirements before the script is written.

  7. Claims you can stand behindYour call

    Only the claims you are allowed to make about your product in your market.

    Results, ingredients, health and price claims are yours to check. A platform can remove an ad for a claim it cannot back.

  8. How you will label it as AIYour call

    Whether your post will carry the platform's AI label, a line in the caption, or both.

    TikTok, Meta and Snapchat each set their own rules for AI content. The platform policy guide linked below quotes them.

  9. Files on Fiverr, not outside links

    Attach every file to the order or to the order chat on Fiverr.

    Links to sites outside Fiverr are not opened by our order process, so a file shared only that way can be missed.

Questions we get asked

Do I need to write the script?

No. The script is written from your brief and shared with you on the order page before production starts. Lines it must include go in the brief.

What if I do not know my ideal customer yet?

Describe who buys from you today, or who you want to reach. If the brief does not say, we ask in the order chat before the script is written.

Can I send a link instead of photos?

Attach photos to the order on Fiverr. Links to sites outside Fiverr are not opened by our order process, so photos attached on Fiverr are the way to make sure your product is seen.

Who decides how the ad is labelled as AI?

You do, as the advertiser who runs it. Each platform sets its own rules, and our platform policy guide quotes them with the date each page was read.

What happens after I send the brief?

The script is written from it and shared with you on the order page before production starts. The revisions included in your package still apply after delivery.
