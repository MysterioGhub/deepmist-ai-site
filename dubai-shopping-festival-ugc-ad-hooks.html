Dubai Shopping Festival

Free Dubai Shopping Festival hooks for retail video ads.

Opening lines for brands advertising around Dubai Shopping Festival, grouped by the kind of ad you are making: fashion, beauty and perfume, tech upgrades, and visitors shopping in the city. They are free to use in your own ads, with no sign-up. If you want us to make the ad, every frame is AI-generated and disclosed.

Planning note Dubai Shopping Festival runs from 4 December 2026 to 17 January 2027, according to Visit Dubai. Whether to take part is each brand's own choice, and each brand sets its own offers, so confirm your own dates and terms before you schedule a festival ad.

During the festival, feeds fill with crowds, fireworks and the same sale banner. An ad that shows your product being chosen, tried on or opened reads as useful instead of loud. Brief it early, so the creative is made and checked well before the festival opens instead of rushed.

The hooks

Fashion and the fitting room

For fashion, shoe and accessory brands whose customers try before they buy.

  • I went in for a jacket and left knowing my actual size.
  • The outfit I almost walked past on the way out.
  • What this dress looks like after a full day of walking.
  • I tried it on in the shop. This is how it looks at home.

Beauty and perfume

For beauty, skincare and fragrance brands where the tester decides the sale.

  • The scent I kept going back to sniff on my wrist.
  • I asked the counter what suits my skin. This is what stuck.
  • Why this lipstick survived a long day out in the heat.
  • The bottle I bought after testing it on my wrist all afternoon.

Tech upgrades

For electronics brands whose buyers have been waiting for the right moment to upgrade.

  • My old headphones finally gave up. Here is what replaced them.
  • I held all of these in my hand before I chose this.
  • The laptop bag that actually fits the laptop.
  • What changed when I stopped living with a cracked phone screen.

Visiting the city

For brands serving visitors and families who come to Dubai to shop during the festival.

  • What I packed an empty suitcase for.
  • The gift I bought here for everyone waiting at home.
  • Shopping with the whole family without losing anyone.
  • The thing I came back for after the trip ended.

Writing for the festival

Show the product, not the crowd

Festival feeds fill with mall crowds and fireworks. Keep the camera on your product in use, so the viewer knows what you sell before they scroll on.

Never invent an offer

Only show a price, a discount or a raffle that your brand has actually confirmed. If the terms are not set yet, make the ad about the product instead.

Arabic written natively

Gulf viewers hear a translated script straight away. Write the Arabic version on its own terms instead of converting the English line.

Ready before it opens

Festival ads take time to make and check. Have the creative ready well before the festival opens, and confirm the dates your own offer runs.
