UAE National Day

Free National Day hooks for brand video ads.

Opening lines for brands that want to mark UAE National Day without shouting, grouped by the kind of ad you are making: made here, gathering, gifting and the holiday break. They are free to use in your own ads, with no sign-up. If you want us to make the ad, every frame is AI-generated and disclosed.

Planning note The UAE marks National Day on 2 December every year. The official public holiday dates are announced each year, so confirm them before you book media or promise a delivery around the holiday.

Around the holiday every feed carries the same celebration post. An ad that shows something real about your product and the people who use it here reads as warmer than another generic greeting. Brief it early, so the creative is made and checked well before the holiday instead of rushed.

The hooks

Made here

For brands that make, roast, bake, blend or design their product in the Emirates.

  • This was made a short drive from where you are sitting.
  • Here is the workshop behind the thing you keep buying.
  • We started this in a small kitchen here, and this is why.
  • Every batch of this is blended right here in the Emirates.

Gathering

For food, coffee, sweets and home brands that sit at the centre of a family visit.

  • The tray that always comes out when the family arrives.
  • Setting the table for everyone this holiday, and this goes in the middle.
  • What we serve when the whole majlis is full.
  • The coffee my family argues about every holiday.

Gifting

For perfume, sweets and gift sets bought for hosts, colleagues and neighbours.

  • Visiting family this holiday? Do not arrive empty-handed.
  • The gift I bring when I do not know what to bring.
  • Something for the host who already has everything.
  • This is how I say thank you to my neighbours.

The holiday break

For travel, leisure, fashion and home brands that fit how people spend the days off.

  • Here is my plan for the holiday, and it starts with this.
  • Staying in the city this holiday? This is how I make it feel away.
  • Packed for the desert, and this came with me.
  • The outfit I saved for the holiday gatherings.

Writing for National Day

Leave the symbols alone

Keep national symbols, emblems and leaders out of a product ad. Show your product and the people who use it, and let the occasion stay in the background.

Warm, not a slogan

Brands earn attention here with a real moment, like a visit, a gift or a day off, rather than slogans. A calm, specific line ages better than a greeting.

Arabic written natively

Gulf viewers hear a translated script straight away. Write the Arabic version on its own terms instead of converting the English line.

Ready before the holiday

The holiday dates are confirmed officially each year. Have the creative made and checked early, then schedule it when the dates are announced.
